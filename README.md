## Overview
This compendium provides a structured, categorized register of peer-reviewed randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and real-world evidence (RWE) evaluating the Transcendental Meditation (TM) technique. It synthesizes empirical clinical and observational evidence across cardiovascular health, cardiometabolic disease, hypertension, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), psychological resilience, neurophysiology, cognitive performance, educational outcomes, healthcare economics, and recidivism rehabilitation.

## Scope of the Compendium
- **Part 1 / Volume 1A (RCTs):** Randomized controlled trials covering cardiovascular endpoints, clinician-rated PTSD, workplace burnout, fluid intelligence, and institutional recidivism.
- **Part 2 / Volume 1B (Longitudinal & RWE):** Actuarial health insurance analyses, multi-year developmental tracking, provincial single-payer healthcare studies, and longitudinal prison rehabilitation data.

## Key Clinical & Research Areas Included
- **Cardiovascular & Cardiometabolic Endpoints:** Long-term survival analysis, myocardial infarction, stroke, carotid atherosclerosis regression, left ventricular mass index (LVMI), insulin resistance (HOMA-IR), and blood pressure regulation.
- **Psychological Health & Trauma:** Clinical trials evaluating clinician-rated PTSD (CAPS), trait anxiety meta-analyses, and depression.
- **Workplace & Clinician Burnout:** Interventions targeting emotional exhaustion and frontline healthcare worker stress.
- **Cognitive & Educational Outcomes:** Fluid intelligence, bilateral EEG coherence, executive function, and academic achievement in adolescents.
- **Substance Abuse & Criminal Rehabilitation:** Long-term recidivism reduction, parole outcomes, and institutional rule infractions.
- **Healthcare Economics & Actuarial Data:** Multi-year hospitalisation and physician payment claims analyses.
