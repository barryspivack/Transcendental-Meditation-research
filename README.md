## Overview
This compendium provides a structured, categorized register of peer-reviewed randomized controlled trials (RCTs) evaluating the Transcendental Meditation (TM) technique. It synthesizes empirical clinical evidence across cardiovascular health, cardiometabolic disease, hypertension, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), psychological resilience, neurophysiology, cognitive performance, educational outcomes, and recidivism rehabilitation.

## Key Clinical Areas Included
- **Cardiovascular & Cardiometabolic Endpoints:** Long-term survival analysis, myocardial infarction, stroke, carotid atherosclerosis regression, left ventricular mass index (LVMI), insulin resistance (HOMA-IR), and blood pressure regulation.
- **Psychological Health & Trauma:** Clinical trials evaluating clinician-rated PTSD (CAPS), trait anxiety meta-analyses, and depression.
- **Workplace & Clinician Burnout:** Interventions targeting emotional exhaustion and frontline healthcare worker stress.
- **Cognitive & Educational Outcomes:** Fluid intelligence, bilateral EEG coherence, executive function, and academic achievement in adolescents.
- **Substance Abuse & Criminal Rehabilitation:** Long-term recidivism reduction and institutional rule infractions.
